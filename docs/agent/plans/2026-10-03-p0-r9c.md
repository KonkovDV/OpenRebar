# P0-r9c — position mass from the exact cut length

Date: 2026-10-03. Base: `main` after `5eefe56`.

## What is wrong

`PositionAssigner`, `LayerReportBuilder`, and `CsvScheduleExporter` turn each bar length into an `int` millimetre and then multiply by the linear mass. The certificate `summary.totalMassKg` already uses the exact cut length. The schedule does not.

PR #133 (`76df549`, base `45b63cc`) describes this with the pre-inset simple-slab: 27 × 6565.49 mm and 437.852 kg. That slab is gone. A free edge now insets the axis by `max(coverEdgeMm, coverMm + d/2)`. For Ø20 and cover 30 mm that inset is 40 mm, the catalogue length is 6485 mm, and the certificate mass is 432.5171065322226 kg.

## Figures that change

simple-slab, BottomX, 27 × Ø20 A500C, shape H. Linear mass is the table value already used by the certificate (2.470 kg/m). Exact cut length of the set is 175108.1402964464 mm.

| Figure | Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lengthMm` | 6485 | 6485 | Catalogue length stays rounded to 1 mm. |
| Position `massPerPieceKg` | 16.017950000000003 | 16.019152093786026 | Average of the exact bar lengths, not 6485 mm. |
| Position `totalMassKg` and layer `massKg` | 432.48465000000004 | 432.51710653222267 | Sum of the 27 exact bar lengths. |
| `summary.totalMassKg` | 432.5171065322226 | 432.5171065322226 | Unchanged. One multiplication of the zone length. The last bit differs from the position sum by about 7e-14 kg. |
| CSV per-piece cell | 16.02 | 16.02 | Both masses print as 16.02 kg at two decimals. |
| CSV steel total | 432.48 | 432.52 | 432.5171065322226 printed at two decimals. |
| Purchased mass, waste, bar count, areas | unchanged | unchanged | The stock bar is still one 9000 mm piece. |

DXF and PNG simple-slab share these masses. The PNG file also keeps its `$INSUNITS` difference out of this change.

## Not in this change

Hook tails, Revit bend geometry, maximum spacing, and effective depth stay as they are. PR #133 is not merged: its description and base commit do not match this tree.
