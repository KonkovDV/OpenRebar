# OpenRebar and the 2026 literature

A reading list for the six places where research touches OpenRebar. Every source below was resolved on 20 October 2026: a DOI against Crossref, an arXiv id against arXiv, a page against the page itself. A figure appears only where the paper's own abstract prints it. What a paper could mean for OpenRebar is our reading, and it says so. Where this page and the code disagree, the code wins.

| Axis | OpenRebar today | What the papers do |
|---|---|---|
| A. Image reading | A PNG is read by exact legend color. The U-Net code has no checkpoint | Promptable segmenters, plain-ViT segmenters, detector boxes that prompt a segmenter |
| B. Layout | Zone grid, even spacing, added bars between the mesh | Graph neural networks as surrogates inside metaheuristics; reinforcement learning |
| C. Cutting | Column generation on HiGHS with a best-fit floor; the house slab bound is `Proven` | Pointer networks trained by reinforcement learning; learned cut selection |
| D. IFC | IFC4 bar type, swept disk, placement; an IDS entity check in CI | IFC 4.3.2.0; couplers as `IfcMechanicalFastener` |
| E. Code checking | SP 63 tables with clause and quote; deterministic | Language-model agents that read code text; parametric slab pipelines |
| F. Physics | Crack width and deflection stay `NotEvaluated` on area-only input | Physics-informed networks for beams |

## A. Image reading

**OpenRebar today.** Without `--ml-url`, a PNG is read by an exact match to the legend color. `ml/` holds an `IsolineUNet` and an empty model manifest. Segmentation accuracy has not been measured.

- **SAM 2** — Ravi et al., arXiv:2408.00714. Promptable segmentation for images and video. On images the abstract reports it more accurate and 6× faster than SAM; in video, better accuracy with 3× fewer interactions. *Our reading:* two clicks on the slab corners could stand in for `--px-per-mm` and `--origin-px`.
- **EoMT** — Kerssies et al., CVPR 2025, doi:10.1109/CVPR52734.2025.02356. A plain ViT used directly as the segmenter, without an adapter, a pixel decoder, or a Transformer decoder. Accuracy similar to models that keep those parts, and up to 4× faster with ViT-L.
- **LUMA** — Nauen et al., arXiv:2607.00687. A lightweight mask head that matches EoMT at lower cost. Across 20 backbones on ADE20K and Cityscapes, plain ViT holds the throughput front, and the pretraining objective, not the architecture, governs quality.
- **MEP-YOLOv11 + SAM** — Zhang et al., *Buildings* 16, 794 (2026), doi:10.3390/buildings16040794. Detector boxes prompt SAM. On concrete cracks: accuracy 95.98 %, precision 92.60 %, IoU 0.77. *Our reading:* the same two stages fit zone boxes turning into zone polygons.

| Step | What | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| ML-A1 | A pretrained ViT backbone in place of the U-Net encoder | The backbone the 2026 benchmarks favor |
| ML-A2 | A SAM 2 prompt on two slab corners | Calibration by click |
| ML-A3 | 50 or more annotated plans, synthetic and real | The first measured accuracy |
| ML-A4 | The head exported to ONNX int8, with CPU latency measured | Proof that it runs offline |

## B. Layout

**OpenRebar today.** A deterministic zone grid: even spacing, with added bars between the background mesh. No learning in the loop.

- **GNN + exploratory genetic algorithms** — Li, Liu, Wong, Gan, *Automation in Construction* 146, 104677 (2023), doi:10.1016/j.autcon.2022.104677. A graph neural network guides the search for steel reinforcement designs.
- **DfMA rebar optimization for slabs** — Li, Lin, Wang, Hu, *J. Comput. Civ. Eng.* 40, 04026053 (2026), doi:10.1061/JCCEE5.CPENG-7477. Graph neural networks with metaheuristics for RC slabs: the same problem OpenRebar solves.
- **Constructability-aware PIGNN** — Verduzco, Cheng, Li, Wang, *Automation in Construction* 182, 106760 (2026), doi:10.1016/j.autcon.2026.106760. A physics-informed graph network as a surrogate for rebar design in beams.
- **Low-carbon beams by DRL** — Hosseinzadeh, Dehestani, *Scientific Reports* 15, 33143 (2025), doi:10.1038/s41598-025-18543-4. Deep reinforcement learning for RC beam design with a low-carbon objective.
- **Shear walls by RL** — Zhao, Ma, Cho, Hwang, *Engineering Structures* 353, 122272 (2026), doi:10.1016/j.engstruct.2026.122272. Reinforcement learning designs shear walls with code compliance.

| Step | What | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| GNN-B1 | A zone graph in Domain: zones as nodes, adjacency and laps as edges | The ground for any graph model; no learning needed |
| GNN-B2 | A graph surrogate for mass and splice count | Fast scoring without a full run |
| GNN-B3 | A learned choice of diameter and spacing for added bars | A layout search under the area check |
| GNN-B4 | The area check as a differentiable penalty | A learned layout that respects A<sub>s,prov</sub> ≥ A<sub>s,req</sub> |

Every learned step waits for a corpus of real plans.

## C. Cutting

**OpenRebar today.** Column generation: a restricted master LP on HiGHS, bounded-knapsack pricing, and largest-remainder rounding with repair. `BestFitDecreasingOptimizer` is the floor. On the house slab the dual bound is `Proven` with a gap of 0. Its 29.9 % waste is the shape of the job: each 6305 mm piece comes from a 9000 mm bar.

- **DRL-CSP** — Fang, Rao, Luo, Xu, *Mathematics* 11, 1028 (2023), doi:10.3390/math11041028. A pointer-network policy trained by model-free RL; an approximate satisfactory solution on 82 instances of 3 data sets in a very short time. *Our reading:* where the bound is already proven there is nothing to win; a policy pays off on large jobs and online re-planning.
- **Pointer-network A2C for bin packing** — Huang, Wang, Pu, Li, *Scientific Reports* (2026), doi:10.1038/s41598-026-63499-8. One-dimensional bin packing for conveyor logistics, with heuristics alongside the policy.
- **RL for Benders decomposition** — Cai, Yu, arXiv:2605.06516. A policy trained with REINFORCE picks the cuts; tested on electric-vehicle charging-station location against plain Benders and a supervised selector. *Our reading:* column generation is the dual side of the same decomposition, so a learned selector could pick columns.

| Step | What | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| CSP-C1 | Waste and purchase cost in the report: `wastePercent`, `estimatedCost` | Done |
| CSP-C2 | The best-fit floor under its own name | Done |
| CSP-C3 | Lap positions as variables of the cutting problem | The r9 worked example: 216 m bought instead of 243 m, with 15 % more steel installed |
| CSP-C4 | A learned policy for jobs above a few hundred pieces | Speed where the LP grows |

## D. IFC

**OpenRebar today.** IFC4 with one bar type per diameter and steel class, an `IfcSweptDiskSolid` of radius d/2, and a placement on the axis. CI runs an entity check against `tools/ci/ids/openrebar.ids`. A lap is drawn as an offset piece; no splice entity is written. A bottom hook bends up into the slab and a top hook bends down into it. On the house slab the hook crown sits on the cover line, 40 mm from the free face. The semicircle still crosses the top face: the axis is at 40 mm, the rise is 180 mm, and the bar surface leaves by 10 mm. The report names that `hookOutsideConcrete`.

- **IFC 4.3.2.0 (IFC4X3_ADD2)** — buildingSMART, [standards.buildingsmart.org/IFC/RELEASE/IFC4_3](https://standards.buildingsmart.org/IFC/RELEASE/IFC4_3/). `IfcReinforcingBarType` dates from IFC4; `IfcReinforcingBar` from IFC2x2, with its occurrence attributes moved to the type in IFC4. `IfcMechanicalFastenerTypeEnum` carries `COUPLER`.
- **IDS 1.0** — buildingSMART. Machine-readable requirements for an IFC file. This repository already checks its examples against one.
- **Allplan 2026** — [AEC Magazine](https://aecmag.com/bim/allplan-unveils-2026-products/). Enhanced automation of column reinforcement design; single-click horizontal or vertical priorities for wall reinforcement. *Our reading:* columns and walls are covered there; a slab laid from an area map is where OpenRebar works.
- **MA-REBARS 2026** — MartinAddin, [Autodesk Marketplace](https://marketplace.autodesk.com/apps/d0bff47a-6adc-4d80-878a-ca18bb5b653f). A commercial Revit extension for everyday rebar detailing, with self-checking tools.

| Step | What | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| IFC-D1 | Bar type, body, and placement | Done |
| IFC-D2 | An IDS entity check in CI | Done |
| IFC-D3 | Couplers as `IfcMechanicalFastener` with `COUPLER` | A coupler another program can read |
| IFC-D4 | A round trip through the Revit IFC importer | A tested claim instead of an assumed one |
| IFC-D5 | The semicircle kept inside the slab depth | The plan crown is on the cover line; the rise still leaves the top face |

## E. Code checking

**OpenRebar today.** SP 63.13330.2018 in versioned JSON tables, each value with its clause and a quote, traced in [NORMATIVE_TRACEABILITY.md](NORMATIVE_TRACEABILITY.md). The checks are deterministic. A person answers the stop questions in `docs/agent/decisions/DECISIONS.md`.

- **Multi-agent LLM design of RC structures** — Chen, Bao, *Automation in Construction* 177, 106331 (2025), doi:10.1016/j.autcon.2025.106331. Language-model agents produce code-compliant designs of reinforced concrete structures.
- **LLM code checking in BIM** — Madireddy, Gao, Din, Kim, arXiv:2506.20551. GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Llama connected to Revit read building codes, write Python scripts, and run semi-automated checks on a house and an office.
- **A Python pipeline for flat slabs** — Triber, Iker, Szép, Gosztola, *Buildings* 16, 3813 (2026), doi:10.3390/buildings16193813. Geometry, a finite element model driven through a commercial program's COM API, Eurocode combinations, reinforcement, and checks in one notebook. A percentile-based zoning turns element-wise demand into a constructable layout. Bottom reinforcement agrees within 10 %; punching shear matches exactly at v<sub>Rd,c</sub> = 0.399 MPa. *Our reading:* the nearest neighbor on this list. OpenRebar does not smooth or percentile the demand; it lays bars on the map as drawn.
- **SEI Structures Congress 2026** — [notes by PES Structural Engineers](https://pesengineers.com/sei-structures-congress-2026). The most accessible starting point is the language model a firm already has, with a well-structured prompt and a narrow scope; prompt design matters more than model choice; the engineer of record stays responsible.

| Step | What | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| LLM-E1 | Retrieval over the SP 63 text | Needs the official text, which this repository has not opened |
| LLM-E2 | A model that drafts a validator from a clause | A draft a person reviews |
| LLM-E3 | A model's answer to a stop question, checked against the primary text | Faster review; the table still changes only with a quote |
| LLM-E4 | The report written as an `IfcApproval` record | The verdict inside the model |

## F. Physics

**OpenRebar today.** The input is required area only, without loads. Crack width and deflection stay `NotEvaluated`.

- **PINNs for beam bending** — Forooghi, Miralizadeh Jalalat, Andriollo, Ruocco, *Engineering with Computers* 42, 138 (2026), doi:10.1007/s00366-026-02375-5. An assessment of physics-informed networks on beam bending problems.
- **Interpretable PINNs for RC beams** — Guo, Guo, Zhou, Xie, *Developments in the Built Environment* 28, 101041 (2026), doi:10.1016/j.dibe.2026.101041. Multi-branch physics-informed networks for structural health assessment of reinforced concrete beams.
